# Ma'din Public school 

Shaping Tomorrow: The Journey of Ma’din Academy
Ma'din Public School, which operates under the umbrella of MADINU SSAQUAFATHIL ISLAMIYYA which was founded in 1997 as a collective of educational institutions and philanthropic initiatives. Today, it has evolved into a dynamic movement for intelligent learning.

Ma’din Academy was founded to accelerate universal endeavours in pursuit of achieving world peace, sustainable development, and educational excellence by translating knowledge into social-minded action.

Ma’din Academy is driven by a noble vision: to make tomorrow better through quality education with uncompromised traditional values, research to redesign innovation to give it increased impact. We are open to talented people, outstanding ideas and boundless resources to co-create the future of the world where people celebrate humanity.

People from all walks of life and all parts of the world have been visiting us to acquire knowledge through the route of value-based education. Our various colleges and extension activities play a vital role, contributing to a progressive society and an enlightened culture.

Foundation and Early Years
Ma’din Academy was established on June 6th, 1997, beginning its journey in a modest Masjid with limited facilities and 183 students. The first institution to be established under the Ma’din banner was Ma’din Shareeath College, located in Malappuram, Melmuri. Rooted in a region with rich culture and tradition, Ma’din emerged as a beacon of hope for a community that was historically underserved in terms of educational opportunities.

Expansion and Growth
Starting in 1998, Ma’din Academy began expanding its reach by adding more institutions. The first addition was the Ma’din Orphanage. In 1999, Ma’din Higher Secondary School, Da’wa College, and the Boarding Madrassa were established, further solidifying the Academy’s commitment to comprehensive education.

The year 2000 marked the inauguration of the Ma’din Computer Center, symbolizing the Academy’s entry into the 21st century with a focus on modern education. This momentum continued in 2001 with the opening of Ma’din Public School. In 2022, Ma’din took a significant step by establishing special schools dedicated to the blind, deaf, and mentally challenged students, demonstrating its commitment to inclusive education.

Current Status
Over the years, Ma’din Academy has consistently grown, with new institutions being added almost every year. Today, Ma’din proudly operates over 50 institutions, each contributing to the holistic development of the community.

Swalath Majlis: The Spiritual Pillar
An integral part of Ma’din Academy is the Swalath Majlis, a monthly spiritual gathering that predates the inception of Ma’din itself. This gathering has been the cornerstone of the Academy’s spiritual and communal life, fostering trust and support among its members.
Milestones and Global Collaborations
Ma’din Academy celebrated its 12th and 24th anniversaries, known as Encomium and Vicennium, respectively. These milestones were pivotal in reshaping Ma’din’s projects and accelerating its growth.

In its journey towards global excellence, Ma’din has established collaborations with various educational and research entities across continents, including Malaysia, Australia, Yemen, UAE, Spain, China, and the USA. These partnerships have resulted in Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) that strengthen Ma’din’s commitment to providing world-class education and research opportunities.
