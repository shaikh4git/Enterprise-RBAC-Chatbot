Mobile Science Exhibition Sparks Curiosity Among Students
August 19, 2023
Ma’din Public School, in partnership with Kozhikode Planetarium, recently hosted an engaging Mobile Science Exhibition that captivated the curiosity of students and parents alike. Over two days, the event drew a crowd of more than five thousand attendees, including students, teachers, and parents, all eager to explore the wonders of science.
The exhibition was inaugurated by Principal Saitalavi Koya, who highlighted the significance of such initiatives in fostering scientific understanding and curiosity. Vice Principal Nurul Amin played a pivotal role in guiding the event, reaffirming the school’s dedication to holistic education.
Program Coordinator Athul took center stage, offering illuminating insights into satellites, their myriad applications in our daily lives, weather forecasting, and even telemedicine. The interactive sessions not only imparted knowledge but also encouraged attendees to ask questions and engage in thoughtful discussions.
The exhibition covered a diverse range of topics, from satellite launches to their practical uses, showcasing the intrinsic link between science and our modern lives. With the participation of figures like Manager Abdurrahman Chemmankadav, Jaafar Saqafi Pazamallur, Vinod Kizhissery, Science Club Coordinator Anwar Meenarkuzhi, and Deepa, the event underscored Ma’din Public School’s commitment to nurturing scientific curiosity and exploration among its students.
