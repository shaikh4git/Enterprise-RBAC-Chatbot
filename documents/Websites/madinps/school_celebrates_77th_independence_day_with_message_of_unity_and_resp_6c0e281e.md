School Celebrates 77th Independence Day with Message of Unity and Responsibility.
August 15, 2023
Ma’din Public School marked the 77th Independence Day of India with a grand celebration that emphasized unity, responsibility, and the well-being of all citizens. The event was held on the school grounds, where the Chairman of Ma’din, Sayyid Ibrahimul Khaleel Al Bukhari, hoisted the national flag.
The flag hoisting ceremony was a symbol of pride and patriotism as Chairman Al Bukhari, in his address to the school assembly, highlighted the significance of the occasion. “The basis of independent India is the happiness of all the citizens of the country,” he stressed. He delivered a poignant message, urging everyone to work towards ensuring the security and stability of the nation. Furthermore, he emphasized the importance of treating all human beings as equals and fostering a spirit of love and unity.
The event was a part of the school’s commitment to nurturing responsible and informed citizens. Principal Saithalavikoya presided over the ceremony, radiating a sense of pride and responsibility among the students and staff.
The celebration featured a series of activities that encapsulated the spirit of patriotism and unity. The school’s Student Police Cadet (SPC) and Junior Red Cross (JRC) units presented an impressive parade that showcased discipline and teamwork. The assembly also took a collective pledge to uphold the values and principles on which the nation was built.
As the sun rose on this special day, the entire assembly reverberated with the soul-stirring notes of the national anthem. The anthem echoed the sentiments of freedom and the shared aspirations of millions of Indians.
The event saw the participation of several school dignitaries who shared their thoughts on this momentous occasion. Academic Director Noufal Kodoor, Vice Principal Nurul Amin, Manager Abdurrahiman Chemmankadav, Jafar Sakhafi Pazamallur, and Abu Tahir Adany delivered inspiring speeches that encouraged the students to embrace their roles as responsible citizens and to actively contribute to the growth and harmony of the nation.
Ma’din Public School’s Independence Day celebration captured the essence of unity, responsibility, and patriotism. It was a reminder to all that the freedom gained through countless sacrifices carries with it the duty to ensure the nation’s progress and the well-being of every citizen.
