School Embarks on New Academic Year with ‘Tilawa’ Quran Study Launch
June 6, 2023
In a momentous event that marked the beginning of a new academic year, Ma’din Public School inaugurated its annual Quran study program, known as ‘Tilawa’. The event was a testament to the school’s commitment to nurturing spiritual growth and fostering a deep understanding of the Quran among its students.
Led by Ma’din Chairman Sayyid Ibrahimul Khaleel Al Bukhari and Syed Ahmadul Kabir Adany Al Bukhari, the Tilawa launch was a gathering of both reverence and excitement. Distinguished guests, educators, and students congregated to celebrate the initiation of Quranic studies for the year.
The event was graced by the presence of various key figures, including Principal Saithalavi Koya, Academic Director Noufal Kodur, and Vice Principal Syed Nurul Amin, among others. Manager Abdurrahman, Abbas Sakhafi, Jafar Sakhafi, Shah Kir Siddiqui, Abdus Samad An Sari, and Muhammad Qudsi also took the stage to share their insights.
Speaking about the significance of the Tilawa program, Chairman Al Bukhari emphasized the importance of Quranic studies in shaping not only academic pursuits but also character and values. He stated, “The Tilawa program is a vital part of our curriculum, ensuring that our students not only gain knowledge but also develop a deep understanding of the Quran’s teachings.”
