Welcome to the School Transfer Certificate (TC) page of Ma'din Public School! Here, we provide you with all the necessary information and steps to apply for a Transfer Certificate and how to download a ready-available Transfer Certificate for Verification through our online portal.
How to Apply for TC
Applying for a Transfer Certificate is a straightforward process at Ma'din Public School. Follow these steps to request your TC:
Visit the School Office: Reach out to the school office to collect the Transfer Certificate application form.
Complete the Form: Fill in the required details accurately and attach any supporting documents as mentioned in the form.
Submit the Form: Submit the completed form along with any required documents to the school office. Please note that there might be a processing fee associated with the TC request.
Processing Time: Once the application is submitted, it will be processed by our administrative team. The Transfer Certificate will be prepared within three working days from the date of submission.
How to Download Ready-Available TC
We understand that a Transfer Certificate (TC) might be required for various purposes. At Ma'din Public School, you can easily download your CV through our online portal:
Access the Online Portal:
Click Here
Fill the required fields: Within the page, enter Admission Number and Date of Birth.
Submit and Download: If the TC is ready, it will be shown in the page. Select the option to download the Transfer Certificate (TC).
Download and Print: Your TC will be available for download in a printable format. Ensure the information is accurate, then print the TC for your use.

For any assistance or queries related to the Transfer Certificate application, please feel free to contact our school office at Student Help Desk.
