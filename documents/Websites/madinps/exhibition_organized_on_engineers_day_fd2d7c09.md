# Exhibition Organized on Engineers’ Day – Madin Public School

Source: https://madinps.edu.in/exhibition-organized-on-engineers-day/

Crawled at: 2026-11-12T02:10:20.430608+00:00

---

As part of Engineers’ Day celebrations, an innovative exhibition was organized at the school. The highlight of the event was a creative display put together by Class 2 students, who showcased various washers they prepared themselves. The young learners demonstrated enthusiasm and hands-on creativity, earning appreciation from teachers and visitors alike for their impressive effort.
