Our academic calendar is thoughtfully planned to provide students, parents, and faculty with a clear and organized schedule for the entire academic year. We believe that effective planning and communication are essential for a successful and enriching educational experience.
Academic Year Overview:
The academic year typically begins in June and ends in March, spanning 10 months.
It is divided into two terms to facilitate a comprehensive learning experience.
We adhere to the guidelines set by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), India, for all academic activities.
