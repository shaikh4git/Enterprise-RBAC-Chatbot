Prioritizing Safety and Security at Ma'din Public School
At School, we value the safety and security of our students and staff above all else
We have taken extensive measures to ensure that everyone on campus feels safe and protected. Our school employs 24 x 7 security guards, and we have installed CCTV surveillance to monitor all areas of the campus. These measures enable our students and staff to focus on learning and growing in a secure environment. We believe that safety and security are fundamental to a successful educational experience, and we are committed to providing it for all who attend our school.
