Sayyid Ibrahimul Khaleel Al Bukhari
Founder and Chairman
Sayyid Ibraheemul Khaleel Al Bukhari is one of the India’s most recognized Muslim personalities. He has traveled the world, lectured to thousands, and composed a number of works spanning education and contemporary issues.
Sayyid Bukhari is founder and chairman of Ma’din Academy, established in 1997, aims the apex at the forefront of learning, teaching and research. It has now grown into a mass movement of smart learning with more than 45 educational and various other institutions with 25,000 students under its vast crown.
He acts in following positions as General Secretary of Kerala Muslim Jama’at the umbrella body of various Muslim organizations, Vice President of Islamic Educational Board of India, New Delhi. It runs educational institutions all over India and various Asian and African countries, Chairman, Gender Development Centre, Kerala: It is a centre of excellence in imparting lifelong training for women.
As the chairman of Ma'din Academy, I am blessed to interact with countless students who are filled with hidden talents just waiting to be discovered. Encouraging and witnessing their growth has been one of my greatest joys since founding Ma'din.
