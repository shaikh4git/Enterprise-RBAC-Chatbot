Smart Classroom
Our Smart Classroom is a technology-driven approach to learning that enhances the teaching and learning experience in a classroom environment.
With digital boards, projectors, and other devices, we create an interactive and engaging learning experience that helps students achieve their full potential. Our teachers use these tools to create dynamic lessons that incorporate multimedia elements such as videos, images, and animations. This approach helps students become more engaged in their learning and helps them retain information better. Join us at Ma'din Public School and experience the benefits of a Smart Classroom!

Atal Tinkering Lab
Ma’din Public School’s Atal Tinkering Lab is a state-of-the-art facility that provides students with a unique opportunity to engage in hands-on learning experiences.
The lab is equipped with cutting-edge technology and tools that allow students to explore the fields of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) in a fun and interactive way. The lab offers a variety of activities and projects that promote creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills. It is a space where students can unleash their potential and develop a passion for innovation and entrepreneurship. The Atal Tinkering Lab is a testament to Ma’din Public School’s commitment to providing its students with the best education possible.

Science Lab
School's Science Lab is a state-of-the-art facility that provides students with a hands-on learning experience in the field of science.
The lab is equipped with modern equipment and resources that allow students to conduct experiments and explore scientific concepts in a safe and controlled environment. With the guidance of experienced and qualified teachers, students are encouraged to develop their critical thinking skills and creativity by working on various projects and research activities. The Science Lab at Ma'din Public School is an integral part of the school's curriculum, providing students with a solid foundation in science and preparing them for future academic and career pursuits.

Library
Ma’din Public School Library is an integral part of the school’s academic and cultural fabric.
It is a vibrant learning space that encourages students to develop a lifelong love of reading and learning. The library boasts an impressive collection of books, magazines, and journals that cover a wide range of topics, from literature and history to science and technology. Students have access to a variety of resources, including online databases and e-books, to support their research and learning needs. The library also provides a welcoming and comfortable environment for students to study, collaborate, and engage in intellectual discussions. With its diverse collection of resources and commitment to fostering a love of learning, Ma’din Public School’s Library is an essential resource for students and a valuable asset to the school community.

IT Lab
School’s ICT Computer Lab is a state-of-the-art facility that provides students with access to the latest computer applications and training.
The lab is equipped with high-speed internet connectivity, modern workstations, and advanced multimedia tools. The lab is designed to facilitate the learning of computer skills and to provide students with an environment that encourages creativity and innovation. The lab is staffed by experienced professionals who are dedicated to helping students achieve their full potential. With its cutting-edge technology and expert staff, Ma’din Public School’s ICT Computer Lab provides students with the tools they need to succeed in today’s digital world.
