We are excited to welcome new students into our vibrant learning community. Below are the details for enrolling your child at Ma'din Public School:
Enrollment Process:
Parents can conveniently enroll their child online through our admission portal. Please
click here
to access the online admission form.
Admission Open for:
Pre KG, LKG, and UKG
1st to 8th Standard
Plus One Science Stream
Apply Online
Confirmation Process:
Once you submit the online application, our school will review your application and contact you for confirmation. Please ensure to provide accurate information and contact details in the application form.
Physical Verification and Confirmation:
Upon receiving confirmation, students and parents are required to visit the admission office on the scheduled date for physical verification and final admission confirmation. Please bring the following documents for verification:
Student's Recent Passport-sized Photo
Student's Identity Card (Adhar Card)
Student's Birth Certificate
Parents' ID Card
Guardian's Consent Letter (if biological parent is not present)
Note:
Admission is subject to availability of seats and meeting eligibility criteria as per school policies.
For any inquiries or assistance regarding the admission process, please feel free to contact us at +91 907262 1616.
We look forward to welcoming your child to Ma'din Public School and providing them with a nurturing and enriching educational experience.
