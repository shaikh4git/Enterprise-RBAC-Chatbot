Madin Public School
Ma’din EDU: Student Management App
Welcome to the Ma'din Public School Student Management App, also known as "Ma'din EDU"! We are delighted to introduce our mobile application, designed to enhance the communication and engagement between parents and our school. This app allows you to stay informed about your child's academic progress, attendance, and other important school-related information.
Key Features
Key Features
View your child's attendance records.
Access report cards and academic performance updates.
Receive important notifications and alerts from the school.
Stay up-to-date with school events and announcements.
Communicate with teachers and school staff more effectively.
Download the App
You can download the Ma'din EDU app on your mobile device. Simply click on the appropriate link below to get started:
Download from the App Store (iOS)
Download from the Play Store (Android)
How to Install
Click on the respective App Store or Play Store link above.
You will be redirected to the app's download page on the App Store or Play Store.
Click the "Install" or "Get" button.
Follow the on-screen instructions to download and install the app on your device.
Once installed, open the app and log in using the credentials provided by the school.
Stay Connected
We encourage all parents to download and install the Ma'din EDU app to stay connected and involved in your child's educational journey. If you have any questions or encounter any issues during the installation or use of the app, please contact our school's IT department for assistance.
Thank you for choosing Ma'din Public School for your child's education. We look forward to improving our communication and collaboration through the Ma'din EDU mobile app.
If you have any further questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
